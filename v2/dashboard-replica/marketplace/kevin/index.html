Before You Get Started

Please read the following disclosures. This takes about a minute.

Item 1. Who We Are

AssetAlign is a financial wellness enablement platform, operated by ABTW Corporation, which is registered as an investment adviser with the Massachusetts Securities Division. We connect you with the right financial solutions, efficiently — whether that's a vetted independent advisor or a trusted partner for insurance, estate planning, tax prep, or debt management. Advisors available through our platform are independent professionals, registered as investment advisers or broker-dealers in their own right; AssetAlign itself does not manage your investments or provide investment, legal, or tax advice directly.

Item 2. Align Credits

Align Credits are a loyalty reward issued solely by AssetAlign. They carry no monetary value outside the AssetAlign platform, cannot be exchanged for cash, and may only be redeemed for services in the AssetAlign Exchange. Credits tied to a real partner transaction (like a completed insurance quote) are available to use the moment you earn them; other credits vest 90 days after being earned. Credits are non-transferable.

Because you can earn and spend credits across different services, they may encourage you to try something you wouldn't otherwise — make each choice on its own merits, not your credit balance.

Item 3. Your Information

By using this platform, you consent to AssetAlign collecting and using your financial profile information — including goals, income range, and insurance information you provide — to facilitate advisor matching and partner recommendations. We may share relevant profile information with matched advisors and Exchange partners solely to facilitate service delivery. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Item 4. No Guarantee

AssetAlign does not guarantee the suitability, performance, qualifications, or conduct of any matched advisor or Exchange partner. Advisor and partner matching is based on profile compatibility and is for informational purposes only. All financial decisions remain solely your responsibility.

Item 5. Our Registration

ABTW Corporation (d/b/a AssetAlign) is registered as an investment adviser with the Massachusetts Securities Division and is not registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). You can independently verify our registration and review our Form ADV at adviserinfo.sec.gov, a free public tool covering both state- and SEC-registered advisers.

Item 6. How AssetAlign Makes Money

AssetAlign may receive compensation — including referral fees and ongoing revenue share — from the financial advisors and Exchange partners (insurance, mortgage, estate planning, tax, and debt services) you connect with. This doesn't add to your cost. It may give us an incentive to favor advisors or partners who pay us more, so the decision to work with any of them is always yours to make independently. Mortgage-related compensation follows the additional rules set by the federal Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA).

Item 7. Questions

If you have questions about these disclosures or our compensation arrangements, please contact us at compliance@assetalign.net before proceeding. The complete disclosure, including how we work with employers, is always available via the "Disclosures" link.
