Hi team,

Quick one from me today.

Take a second and think about everywhere your money actually lives right now.

A checking account. Maybe a savings account somewhere else. A 401(k) from this job, maybe another from a old one. A mortgage. A couple of insurance policies. A credit card or two you're tracking separately.
Bank 401(k) Brokerage Mortgage Life insurance Auto insurance Home insurance Credit cards

Most of us are juggling eight or more separate logins just to answer one simple question: "how am I actually doing?" That's not a personal failing — it's just how financial life works today. Nobody hands you a way to see it all in one place.

AssetAlign is now available to everyone at Meridian Industries. It's a voluntary program, completely free to use, and it's run entirely by AssetAlign — not by us. We're simply making sure you know it exists. Whether or not you use it is entirely up to you.

Here's what it actually does: you tell it which providers you already use — takes about two minutes — and it gives you one real, organized picture of your financial life instead of six different apps. As you go, you'll pick up Align Credits along the way, which convert into real value with AssetAlign's partners.

And when you're ready — no pressure, no timeline — AssetAlign can also connect you with a vetted, independent, fiduciary financial advisor to build you an actual plan. That's there when it's useful to you. It's not something you have to do today, and it's not the first step. Getting organized is the first step. A plan is what comes next, once you know where you stand.

Get organized →
