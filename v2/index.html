V2 — the redesign track

A full fork of the live skunkworks replicas, built to rework the product from the inside out around a real positioning shift — not a new mockup on the side. V1 stays exactly where it is for side-by-side comparison; nothing here touches it.

The shift being tested: lead with AssetAlign as a decision-making / organization platform — the Financial Passport, "never fill out documents again," rewards running quietly underneath — instead of leading with advisor-matching.

Advisor-matching moves later, not out: once AssetAlign actually knows the member, it progressively advocates for a real conversation with a fiduciary advisor. "Plan" is always paired with "fiduciary" — the platform organizes you and gets you to a plan; the advisor writes it.
